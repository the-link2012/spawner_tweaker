Improvements from the previous version (Spawner Tweaker v2.5.0 for 26.3):

Features:
-Added a system that detects excess light around spawners and warns the tweaker
 -On by default, displays when within the spawner range
 -Options for a particle, block display, or disable
 -Limited to checking 1,000 blocks per spawner when active (this system isn't cheap, but should be lightweight for 90% of areas)

Bugs:
-Fixed a bug where the spawn box was not turning grey when the player left the radius
